# Monthly Loss Forecasting

## Purpose

The forecasting team produces a monthly loss estimate for governed review.

## Method

1. Load the approved data extract.
2. Apply the documented transformation.

| Input | Owner | Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claims extract | Forecasting | Monthly |

The formula is $loss = exposure * rate$ and the supporting calculator is offline.
