# Control Evidence Standard

**Reference ID:** STD-CONTROL-EVID-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Applicability:** Documents tagged `controlled_activity`

## Evidence requirements

1. A control **MUST** identify its objective, risk, owner, execution event or frequency, procedure, evidence type, and failure response.
2. Evidence **MUST** identify its producer, storage reference, period/as-of date, and review status.
3. A control failure **MUST** route to an escalation path and record the decision or remediation evidence.
4. Evidence **SHOULD** be retained in the named system rather than only as a screenshot in a document.

## Relationship to retention

This standard describes evidence content. The records-retention policy controls the minimum retention period when the standard is less specific.
