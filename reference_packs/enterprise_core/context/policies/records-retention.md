# Records Retention Policy

**Reference ID:** POL-REC-001
**Effective:** 2026-01-01
**Applicability:** Documents tagged `records` and evidence retained by a governed process

## Policy requirements

1. Approval records, control evidence, exception approvals, and final methodology outputs **MUST** identify a retention basis and retention period.
2. The minimum default retention period for governed operational evidence is **seven years after the end of the fiscal year** in which the evidence was finalized, unless a longer legal, regulatory, or contractual period applies.
3. A shorter period **MUST NOT** override this policy without a named policy exception and approval by Records Management.
4. Retention metadata **MUST** identify the system or record location and the disposal review owner.

## Precedence note

This policy controls minimum retention when a lower-precedence standard provides a shorter operational suggestion. It does not authorize deletion or change a legal hold.
