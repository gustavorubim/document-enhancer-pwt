Turn blocking and high-value findings into one-question-per-item reviewer questions. Deduplicate
semantically equivalent questions, order prerequisites before dependent questions, and include
why the answer matters, evidence, target, expected answer shape, allowed states, and dependencies.
Propose a safe default only for a justified non-factual choice; never propose owners, IDs,
thresholds, approvals, controls, dates, or evidence as facts.
