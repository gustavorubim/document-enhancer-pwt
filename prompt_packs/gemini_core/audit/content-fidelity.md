Audit the enhanced document independently from the rewriter's scratch context. Check source
fidelity, unsupported additions, omitted or weakened requirements, contradictions, provenance,
template/reference compliance, semantic references, table/diagram consistency, unresolved
blockers, and review status. Cite evidence and classify each issue as a deterministic check,
content finding, or approved waiver. Do not rewrite the document in the audit response.
